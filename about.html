About Me

Hello — I'm Mark Pardede. I build websites and web apps end to end, from the design system to deployment. My favorite projects are ones that directly help my clients thrive. From company profile for exposure or landing page to ease their work.

I discovered that I loved technology when I was first introduced to SQL in high school. We still had to use Command Prompt to access it back then. But my journey started in 2016

I got into Singapore's Republic Polytechnic and undertook a study for a Diploma in Information Technology. Studying alone abroad at the age of 18 was exciting as much as it was challenging. I finished my study in 2019 after a quick internship with NEC. But my interest in web development hasn't stopped since

Now, I'm undertaking a program with Apple Developer Academy in Bali, creating apps from the ground up with fellow learners with empathy in mind.

In parallel I take on freelance work. I rebuilt the business website for Selat Malaka Indonesia with a modern design and SEO in mind, improving page performance by 30% — and I'm currently building SettleSmart, a financial-advisory site with Firebase-backed client inquiries.

When the IDE is off, however, I enjoy learning about various things through vidoes, writing fictional things, and tinkering in Godot (Also an IDE, but, you know)

If you'd like to build something together, get in touch.
